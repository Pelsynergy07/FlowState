Install

Running in about ten minutes.

  1. Download the installer

    About 1 GB. One file, FlowStateSetup.exe, straight from GitHub.

  2. If Windows warns you, choose Run anyway

    FlowState isn't code-signed yet, so Windows may show "Windows protected your PC". Click More info, then Run anyway. Every release has a SHA-256 checksum on GitHub if you'd like to verify the file.

  3. Follow the setup window

    It checks your PC and downloads the speech and formatting models once: about 3.1 GB with an NVIDIA graphics card, about 1.3 GB without. You pick a microphone, check your shortcuts and try one real dictation while it downloads.

  4. Talk anywhere

    FlowState lives in the system tray. Hold Ctrl M in any app and start talking. Updates arrive in the tray menu with one click.

On a Mac

  1. Download FlowState-macOS.dmg and drag FlowState into Applications.
  2. Open it. The first time, macOS stops it because it isn't notarized yet: go to System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway.
  3. Setup asks for the microphone, Accessibility and Input Monitoring. Switch each on and FlowState comes back by itself.
  4. FlowState lives in the menu bar. Hold Right ⌥ Option in any app and start talking.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. FlowState is free and open source under the MIT license. There is no account and no paid tier.

Does it work on a Mac?

Yes, on Macs with Apple silicon (M1 or newer) running macOS 14 or later. Speech runs on the Mac's Neural Engine and formatting on its graphics, so text appears in about a second. The one-time model download is about 1.6 GB.

Do I need an NVIDIA graphics card?

Not on a Mac, and not on Windows either. With an NVIDIA graphics card, text appears in about two seconds. On any other PC, FlowState uses smaller models on your processor. It works the same, and text takes a few seconds longer to appear.

Will it change what I said?

No. FlowState adds punctuation and capitals, removes "um" and repeated words, and lays out emails and lists. It never swaps your words for synonyms, rewrites sentences or adds anything you didn't say.

Which apps does it work in?

Any app where you can type: email, Slack, Teams, Word, Notion, VS Code, browsers and AI chats. It pastes where your cursor is, then restores your clipboard.

Does it work offline?

Yes, after the one-time model download in setup. The internet is only used to download models and to check for updates.

Which languages does it support?

FlowState is built and tested for English. On a Mac you can switch to the Whisper speech model in Settings for other languages.

Why do I see a warning when I install it?

FlowState isn't code-signed yet. On Windows, SmartScreen doesn't recognise the installer: click More info, then Run anyway. On a Mac, open System Settings, then Privacy & Security, and click Open Anyway next to FlowState. The code and the builds are public on GitHub, and every release lists SHA-256 checksums.

How do I update or uninstall it?

When a new version is out, "Update available" appears in the tray or menu bar and installs with one click, keeping your settings and models. To uninstall on Windows, use Settings, then Apps, then FlowState. On a Mac, quit FlowState and drag it from Applications to the Trash.
